# Research protocol | UP - The Yogi Years
Research cutoff: 18 October 2026. Period of interest: 19 March 2017 onward, with comparable earlier baselines.

## Editorial method
1. Define each parameter: label, dataset, geographic scope, time period, unit, source, denominator and limitations.
2. Establish a 2014-16 or nearest available pre-2017 baseline where warranted. Use multi-year baselines to reduce outlier effects.
3. Prefer original signed orders, legislative answers, state budget actuals, CAG audit reports, NCRB tables, MoSPI/PLFS, NFHS/NITI reports, ASER and department dashboards with dated snapshots.
4. Apply four distinct source statuses: original survey/audited record; administrative data; attributed official statement; independent reporting/analysis. Do not transform claims into findings.
5. Present comparators fairly: predecessor state governments, other states of similar composition and scale, national benchmarks and within-UP district variation.
6. Separate nominal and constant-price GDP, BE/RE/actuals, physical project announcements and openings, intent/MoU versus commercial production, and reported FIRs versus underlying incidence.
7. Test causality conservatively. Central schemes, inherited capital projects, Covid, macroeconomic change, changing survey methods and local administration affect all outcomes.
8. Where data are not validated, list the pending question and source plan. Avoid invented certainty, fictional quotes, fabricated district-specific numbers and false equivalence.
9. For judicial matters, distinguish allegation, charges, investigation, court proceedings, judgment, appeal and compliance.
10. Preserve date, original link, document version and corrections trail. Invite documented counter-evidence.

## Initial verified context
- PRS UP 2026-27 reports total BE ₹9,12,696 crore, FY2024-25 actual capital outlay ₹1,14,001 crore and FY2026-27 fiscal deficit target 3%.
- NFHS-5 for UP measures stunting at 39.7% in 2020-21 versus 46.3% in NFHS-4; severe wasting at 7.3% versus 6.0%.
- National NITI SDG India Index gives UP 67 in 2023-24 and 42 in 2018. UP’s separate district SDG index reports state aggregate 68.72 in 2023-24; these are **not interchangeable**.
- MPI headcount rate: 37.68% NFHS-4 versus 22.93% NFHS-5 in UP (NITI / UNDP). A 2024 discussion-paper estimate of 5.94 crore people exiting deprivation in UP spans **2013-14 to 2022-23**, not exclusively 2017 onward.
- PRS 2022 assembly analysis: BJP 312 seats in 2017, 255 in 2022; Samajwadi Party 47 and 111 respectively.
- Supreme Court: 13 November 2024 demolition-procedure judgment, case W.P.(C) 295/2022 and connected matters.
